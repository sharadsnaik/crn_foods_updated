Our journey

Built by someone who has spent a career inside Indian FMCG

"CRN Foods is a next-generation FMCG company rooted in purity, innovation and modern technology."

CRN Foods Pvt. Ltd. was founded by Gopi Rajakannu, whose career in India's retail and food industry spans decades and includes a role in shaping several brands that are now household names. That background in food science, manufacturing and consumer behaviour shapes how CRN Foods is run today — with a particular focus on modern processing techniques, including nanotechnology-based methods, aimed at improving freshness, shelf stability and consumer safety across the product range.
