About CRN Foods

A manufacturing partner, not just a factory

CRN Foods Pvt. Ltd. manufactures snacks, namkeen, sweets, chips and ready-to-eat products for brands across India, out of our facility near Dharmapuri, Tamil Nadu.

Our journey

Decades in Indian FMCG, now built into one factory

CRN Foods was founded by Gopi Rajakannu, who has spent decades contributing to India's retail and food ecosystem, including a role in shaping several brands that are now household names. That experience — combined with a close interest in food science, manufacturing and consumer behaviour — is what CRN Foods is built on.

The company's approach leans on modern processing methods, including nanotechnology-based techniques, aimed at improving freshness, shelf stability and consumer safety. The goal is straightforward: a next-generation FMCG manufacturer rooted in purity, innovation and technology, capable of helping other brands create genuinely distinctive Indian food products.

Gopi Rajakannu — Founder, CRN Foods Pvt. Ltd.

What we hold ourselves to

Three commitments behind every batch

01

Quality & freshness

Manufacturing processes are built to hold quality and freshness steady, batch after batch, so what a customer tastes on day one is what they get on day one hundred.

02

Sustainability

Eco-friendly initiatives are worked into our manufacturing processes as standard practice, not an add-on — part of an ongoing effort to reduce environmental impact.

03

Local producers

We source locally wherever we can, which keeps value inside the communities around our facility and gives us tighter control over ingredient quality.

Our certifications sit behind every product we manufacture, private label or otherwise.
